🏗️ SKY ARCH LAB

Think → Test → Design → Critique

SKY ARCH LAB is a digital laboratory for architectural design thinking and experimentation.

It is being developed to help architecture students move beyond simply producing final drawings and instead understand, document, test, and improve the decisions that shape their designs.

The platform brings together architectural design processes, site analysis, building studies, design critique, practical tools, and project development in one digital workspace.

---

🎯 Vision

Architecture is more than creating a beautiful building.

Every design involves decisions:

- Why is the building positioned here?
- Why was this form selected?
- Why is the entrance located there?
- Why were particular materials chosen?
- How does the building respond to climate?
- How do people move through the building?
- What happens when the original assumptions change?

SKY ARCH LAB is designed to make those decisions visible, testable, and easier to improve.

---

The platform follows a continuous design process:

RESEARCH

Rather than focusing only on the final architectural product, SKY ARCH LAB focuses on the journey that produces the design.

🧱 Building Lab

- Space planning

- Ventilation

---

🧠 Critique Lab

A system designed to challenge architectural decisions rather than simply approve them.

Students may be asked questions such as:

«Why did you make this decision?»

«What problem does this design solve?»

«What is the weakness of this approach?»

«What happens if the number of users increases?»

«What alternative did you consider?»

The goal is to encourage critical architectural thinking.

🎨 Design Philosophy

SKY ARCH LAB is intended to feel like a combination of:

Architecture Studio + Digital Laboratory + Personal Workspace

The interface aims to be:

- Modern
- Minimal
- Professional
- Visual
- Responsive
- Easy to navigate
- Suitable for both students and future professionals

---

🌍 Long-Term Goal

The initial project is focused on architecture students, but the long-term vision is broader.

SKY ARCH LAB could eventually become a platform where students can:

Learn → Explore → Experiment → Critique → Improve → Present

The objective is to create a digital environment that encourages better architectural thinking rather than simply providing more architectural information.
