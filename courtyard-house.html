UNBUILT CONCEPT STUDY

Courtyard
House.

A home shaped around an open-air room — exploring how privacy, daylight, ventilation and everyday life can meet at the centre of a residential plan.

DISCIPLINE Residential architecture
DESIGN FOCUS Space, climate and privacy

FIG. 01 SPATIAL STUDY

An abstract spatial study. Not a measured architectural plan.

01 / THE QUESTION

What if the centre of the home was also its most open space?

Conventional room-by-room planning can make outdoor space feel separate from daily life. This concept reverses that relationship: the courtyard becomes the organising element around which rooms, movement and views are arranged.

The aim is not a fixed architectural style. It is a spatial principle that can be adapted to a suitable site, household, budget and local building requirements.

02 / DESIGN BRIEF

A home organised around daily life.

A hypothetical brief establishes the design direction before dimensions, construction systems or a real site are selected.

01

Privacy

Separate quieter private rooms from shared family activities.

02

Connection

Create useful visual and physical connections to the courtyard.

03

Comfort

Investigate daylight, shading and ventilation through design.

04

Adaptability

Allow the room arrangement to respond to occupants and site needs.

03 / SPATIAL ORGANISATION

Read the plan.

04 / ENVIRONMENTAL THINKING

Design with light and air.

The courtyard creates opportunities for environmental design. Actual results depend on orientation, openings, weather, shading, surrounding buildings and detailed design.

01 / LIGHT

Daylight

The open courtyard may introduce daylight to adjacent rooms. Glare and heat gain would need to be evaluated.

02 / AIRFLOW

Ventilation

Opposing openings may support cross-ventilation when wind, room layout and opening positions allow it.

03 / SHADE

Shading

Roof overhangs, screens and planting can be explored to manage sun exposure and improve outdoor comfort.

05 / MATERIAL EXPLORATION

Quiet materials. Clear structure.

The visual language pairs restrained mineral finishes with warm natural textures and planting. These are design directions, not final product specifications.

Mineral surfaces

Explore durable masonry or rendered finishes appropriate to the site.

Warm screens

Investigate timber or other suitable screening systems for shade and privacy.

Landscape

Consider planting and permeable ground where local conditions support them.

06 / NEXT DESIGN ITERATION

From spatial idea to a testable design.

01

02

Confirm the household brief, room requirements and approximate area schedule.

03

Study orientation, sun paths, wind, privacy and neighbouring conditions.

04

Develop measured plans, sections, elevations and a coordinated structural concept.

05

Review the design against applicable codes with qualified professionals before any construction use.

Important project note

Courtyard House is an original conceptual study for portfolio development. The drawings are schematic, not measured or suitable for construction. No site survey, structural calculations, energy analysis, cost plan or regulatory assessment has been completed. The environmental diagrams show design intentions, not verified building performance.
