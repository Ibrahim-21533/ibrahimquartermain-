ABOUT SKY ARCH LAB

Architecture is not just what you design.

It is the thinking, testing, questioning and decisions behind the design.

01 — OUR IDEA

A digital laboratory for architectural thinking.

SKY ARCH LAB is a digital platform created to help architecture students explore, develop and question their design ideas.

Instead of focusing only on the final drawing or presentation, the platform focuses on the process that leads to the final design.

From understanding a site to developing concepts, comparing alternatives and challenging design decisions, SKY ARCH LAB is designed to make the architectural process more deliberate.

02 — THE PROCESS

Think before you draw.

Good architecture is developed through a process of exploration, testing and refinement.

01

Research

Understand the problem, users, context and requirements.

02

Analyse

Examine the site, environment, movement and opportunities.

03

Design

Develop concepts and explore different architectural possibilities.

04

Test

Question your decisions and compare alternative approaches.

05

Critique

Identify weaknesses, conflicts and opportunities for improvement.

06

Refine

Improve the design using what you have learned throughout the process.

OUR VISION

Better decisions.
Better spaces.

SKY ARCH LAB aims to create a digital environment where architecture students can experiment, question their ideas and develop stronger design reasoning.

The goal is not to replace creativity or architectural judgement, but to give students better tools for developing both.

04 — THE FUTURE

From a website to a digital studio.

SKY ARCH LAB is being developed as an evolving project.

Future versions may introduce collaborative projects, intelligent design critique, advanced site analysis, project histories, digital portfolios and other tools for architectural education.

The platform will continue to grow around one principle:

Think → Test → Design → Critique
