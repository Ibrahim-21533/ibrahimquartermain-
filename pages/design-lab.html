01 / DESIGN LAB

Turn ideas into architectural decisions.

The Design Lab is where concepts are developed, tested, questioned and transformed into stronger architectural solutions.

01

Design is a process, not a final drawing.

Every architectural project begins with an idea. The Design Lab helps you move from that first idea toward a clear and defensible design through research, exploration and testing.

THE DESIGN PROCESS

From thought to form.

01

Research

Understand the problem, users, context, requirements and opportunities.

02

Analyse

Break the problem into smaller architectural questions and identify important relationships.

03

Concept

Develop an architectural idea that responds to the information discovered during research.

04

Explore

Produce different possibilities instead of immediately accepting the first solution.

05

Test

Test the proposal against function, space, structure, environment and context.

06

Refine

Identify weaknesses, make decisions and develop the strongest version of the design.

DESIGN QUESTIONS

Before asking “Does it look good?”

Ask whether the design works. Who uses the space? Why is it organised this way? What happens when people move through it? How does the building respond to its site and environment?

EXPLORE

Areas of design thinking.

01

Concept Development

Explore architectural concepts and discover how an idea can influence form, space and experience.

02

Space Planning

Organise spaces according to users, activities, relationships, hierarchy and movement.

03

Form Exploration

Investigate different forms and understand how geometry affects architectural experience.

04

Circulation

Study how people enter, move through, occupy and leave an architectural space.

05

Design Alternatives

Compare multiple solutions before committing to one final architectural direction.

06

Design Decisions

Record why important decisions were made so that the design can be clearly explained and defended.
