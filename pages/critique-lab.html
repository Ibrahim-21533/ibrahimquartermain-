04 / CRITIQUE LAB

Question the design before the design questions you.

The Critique Lab is a space for examining architectural decisions, identifying weaknesses and discovering opportunities for improvement.

01

Criticism is not the enemy of design.

A design becomes stronger when it is questioned. Critique helps reveal assumptions, contradictions and missed opportunities before they become permanent.

CRITIQUE AREAS

Ask better questions.

01

Function

Does the building actually support the activities and users it was designed for?

02

Context

Does the proposal respond meaningfully to its physical, cultural and environmental context?

03

Circulation

Can people understand and navigate the building naturally and efficiently?

04

Structure

Does the structural idea support the architectural concept rather than contradicting it?

05

Environment

How does the building respond to sunlight, wind, climate, comfort and energy use?

06

Experience

What does a person actually experience while approaching, entering and occupying the building?

THE CRITIQUE PROCESS

Question. Compare. Improve.

01

Observe

Look at the proposal objectively before deciding whether it succeeds or fails.

02

Question

Challenge the reasons behind important architectural decisions.

03

Identify

Find specific strengths, weaknesses, conflicts and opportunities.

04

Compare

Consider alternative approaches instead of assuming there is only one solution.

05

Decide

Determine which changes will improve the architectural proposal.
